# Spec Summary (Lite)

Split a vehicle's files into papers under a fixed, grouped category list and photos shown on a new per-vehicle
Photos screen with thumbnails, a full-screen viewer and a cover photo, all on the existing documents storage.
The browser makes a thumbnail and display copy of every image at upload, which fixes HEIC display and strips GPS
from what the app shows. Service records, expenses and issues show their attached papers and photos.
